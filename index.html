Getting started

  1. Get a game you own, ideally as a .chd (convert your discs if needed).
  2. Click Play now → Load a disc → pick your file.
  3. On iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.

💡 A BIOS is optional (the player uses HLE by default). If the screen stays black on Brave/Chrome, GPU acceleration is likely disabled — enable chrome://flags/#ignore-gpu-blocklist, or use Firefox.
